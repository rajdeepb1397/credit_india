## Fed StarBiz Credit Card - Visa

**Official page:** https://www.federal.bank.in/fed-starbiz-credit-card-visa  
**Extracted on:** 2026-05-20

### Official extracted card text

- Features & Benefits
  - Explore newer possibilities of your business with “FED STARBIZ” Credit Card.
  - Fed Starbiz Credit Card is linked to the customer’s Overdraft (OD) or Cash Credit (CC) underlying account.
  - Underlying account limit must be less than 50L.
  - The card offers seamless merchant and online transactions catering to business requirements.
  - Card validity is 5 years, subject to underlying account status.
  - Daily usage limits are up to ₹3 lakhs or the available limit on the underlying account, whichever is lower.
  - Customers can reach out to Business Banking RM or nearest Federal Bank branch to avail Fed StarBiz Credit Card.
  - Repayment is to be done in the underlying account as per process.
  - Applicable charges shall be levied on the card transactions as per agreed MITC.
- Feature details
  - Linked to OD/CC Account: linked to the customer’s existing Overdraft (OD) or Cash Credit (CC) account with limits up to ₹50 lakhs, providing seamless integration with business finances.
  - Mode of transactions: supports POS, Ecom and Tap and Pay Transactions.
  - Enhanced Security: advanced security features such as tokenization and encryption for safe and secure transactions.
  - Wide Acceptance: use the Fed StarBiz card at millions of merchants across India, wherever RuPay & Visa is accepted.
  - Daily Usage Limits: transactions up to ₹3 lakhs per day or the available limit on the underlying account, whichever is lower.
  - No Separate Statements: all card transactions reflect in the underlying OD/CC account, eliminating the need for separate card statements.
- Eligibility
  - Federal Bank OD account holder with limits up to 50 Lacs.
  - Individuals engaged in activities (manufacturing and services) coming under definition of MSMED Act 2006.
  - Constitution: individuals and sole proprietors only to be considered.
- Fees & Charges
  - Issuance Fee: Rs.275 + GST.
  - Annual Fee: Rs.275 + GST.
  - Reissuance Fee: Rs.225 + GST.
- Terms and usage details shown on the page
  - A Credit Card is a physical or virtual payment instrument issued by banks linked to the underlying ODCC account.
  - It allows cardholders to make payments for purchases at POS / Online up to the available limit on the underlying account.
  - Federal Bank Fed StarBiz Credit Cards are offered on RuPay and Visa network.
  - The total card limit is the total sanctioned limit on the underlying ODCC account.
  - Available card limit refers to the current balance limit on the underlying ODCC account.
  - Card PIN is a four-digit code used to verify the card owner and protect against fraud.
  - Fed StarBiz credit card PIN can be generated using soft PIN option at Federal Bank ATM or using Interactive Voice Response (IVR) System.
  - Soft PIN at Federal Bank ATM steps displayed on page: Insert/Swipe Card in ATM → Select Soft PIN/PIN Change → Select Soft PIN → Generate a New Soft PIN → Get Soft PIN on mobile number registered with the Bank, valid for 24 hours → Proceed To Set PIN → Enter Soft PIN → Enter and confirm new PIN → Success/Failure message displayed.
  - IVR PIN generation: dial 04846716700 or 04842846699 from registered mobile number and press 1 to generate card PIN, then enter last 6 digits of card number, date of birth in DDMMYYYY format, expiry date in MMYY format, new 4-digit PIN and re-enter PIN.
  - Card can be activated through IVR by dialling 04846716700 or 04842846699 from registered mobile number.
  - CVV is a 3-digit code printed at the back of the credit card and acts as an additional security layer for online transactions.
  - Contactless card symbol indicates payment without physically swiping or dipping the card, for transactions up to Rs.5,000.
  - Contactless payments can be made at merchants having NFC enabled POS machines.
  - For contactless payment: look for contactless symbol, tap card on the machine, and if transaction amount is greater than Rs.5,000, provide PIN.
  - Renewal card must be applied basis the underlying OD CC account status and limits not exceeding 50 Lacs.
  - Communication address changes in the underlying ODCC account facility are auto-updated in the card.
  - Additional eligibility shown in page FAQ: active OD CC account with limits up to 50 lacs, constitution as individuals/sole proprietors, MSME registered, Indian resident.
  - Application can be submitted at nearest Federal Bank branch.
  - Duly signed application form must mention underlying account number and details.
  - Customer may contact branch staff or Business Banking RM.
  - Query contact numbers shown: 1800-420-1199 / 1800-425-1199 / 0484-2630994.
  - To activate Physical Credit Card for POS/Contactless, PIN must be set first.
  - E-com transactions can be activated through IVR by dialling 04846716700 or 04842846699 from registered mobile number.
  - Online and contactless transactions can be enabled/disabled through IVR by dialling 04846716700 or 04842846699.
  - FedNet users can activate/manage card through FedNet → Card Services → Manage Card.
  - Transaction limits can be managed through FedNet → Card Services → Manage Card → Manage Transaction Limits.
  - Contactless decline due to disabled control can be addressed through IVR or FedNet.
  - The card limit is a function of the underlying OD CC account; OD CC account limit can be increased basis regular process.
  - Fed StarBiz Credit Card comes with Joining Fee or Annual Membership Fee of Rs.275 + applicable taxes.
  - Late payment charges are not applicable because all repayment is to be done in the underlying OD CC account.
  - Replacement fee: Rs.225 + applicable taxes for reissue/replacement.
  - Max limit for contactless payment is Rs.5000 without PIN, and Rs.50000 with PIN.
  - Limits can be referred to on the underlying OD CC accounts.
  - A dispute can be initiated within 30 days of making the transaction.
  - Billing disputes will normally be addressed within 14 working days depending on nature of dispute.
  - Resolution will be intimated through SMS/e-mail or call.
