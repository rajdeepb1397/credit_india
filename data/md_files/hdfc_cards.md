### FAQ details shown

- Fuel Points are earned on retail spends and accelerated on fuel spends only at IndianOil Retail outlets, groceries and utilities as per product features.
- Accelerated 5% Fuel Points benefit is only for non-UPI transactions at IndianOil Retail Outlets.
- IndianOil Credit Card is described as ideal for frequent drivers and helps maximise savings on fuel and utility bills.
- IndianOil XTRAREWARDS Program is an Indian Oil Corporation loyalty programme that awards XTRAREWARDS Points on transactions at IndianOil Retail Outlets and allows free fuel redemption at participating outlets.
- IndianOil HDFC Bank Credit Cardholders get IXRP membership automatically and free. Membership stays valid after card closure or upgrade/migration, but Fuel Points can no longer be converted to XTRAREWARD Points after that.
- Latest mobile number should be updated on IndianOil XTRAREWARDS Membership, otherwise redemption will not process successfully.
- Help channels shown: 1800 1600 / 1800 2600 in India, 022-61606160 overseas, Eva, nearest branch and HDFC Bank Contact Us page.
- If XTRAREWARDS Points are not credited within 72 hours, Fuel Points are credited back into the credit card account within 7-10 business days.
- XRP can be tracked on xtrarewards.com, POS terminal at participating outlets, SMS `XRP <IOCL XRP Card No>` to 9223177998, or IndianOil ONE app.
- Existing XTRAREWARDS customers can apply; existing XR CID is merged with this programme.
- Cardholders are automatically and mandatorily registered with IndianOil XTRAREWARDS Program during card issuance and should receive SMS confirmation with XR CID within 5 business days.
- XR CID is created by IndianOil for free fuel redemption and differs from the Bank customer ID.
- At participating outlets, fuel bill can be paid partly with XRP and partly with IndianOil HDFC Bank Credit Card.
- Fuel Points for previous calendar month's posted transactions can be checked on the current statement or through NetBanking > Cards > Enquiry > Reward Points Redemption > choose IndianOil HDFC Bank Credit Card Number > view total Fuel Points accrued.
- The card can also be used for shopping, with rewards or cashback on other transactions.

- MyCards functions listed: credit card registration and activation, PIN setup, online/contactless controls, transaction viewing/e-statement download and Reward Points check.
- Contactless payments are enabled at retail outlets. In India, contactless payment without PIN is allowed up to ₹5,000 per transaction; PIN is required if amount is higher than or equal to ₹5,000.
